Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Om Creation ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and discloses your information when you use the Arrow Escape : Reveal Rewards mobile application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

  • Device and Usage Data: We automatically collect certain information about your device and how you interact with the App. This includes your IP address, device type, operating system version, unique device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID), and crash reports.
  • In-Game Data: We store your game progress, gallery unlocks, and level completion status locally on your device.
  • Storage Access: To facilitate the core "Download Image" feature, the App requests permission to write to your device's local storage/photo gallery. We do not access, view, or upload your personal photos or files.

2. Third-Party Advertising and Analytics

The App uses third-party advertising networks to provide free content. These networks may collect and use your unique device identifiers and usage data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads. We integrate the following partners:

  • Google AdMob: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543
  • Unity Ads: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • AppLovin MAX: https://legal.applovin.com/privacy/

These third parties may use cookies, web beacons, and other tracking technologies to collect information about your use of the App and other websites or applications across your devices.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the App's functionality.
  • Serve relevant advertisements through our third-party ad network partners.
  • Analyze user engagement and fix technical issues or bugs.
  • Enable the saving of unlocked images to your device's local storage.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We only share non-identifiable, aggregated usage and device data with our trusted advertising and analytics partners listed above to facilitate ad monetization and improve game performance.

5. Children's Privacy

Our App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it immediately.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy and publishing the revised version on our Play Store listing.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at nsgabani@gmail.com.

Disclaimer: This privacy policy is provided as a foundational template. Because privacy laws (such as GDPR, CCPA, and CPRA) and Google Play Store policies update frequently, it is highly recommended to have a legal professional review your final policy before publishing.
